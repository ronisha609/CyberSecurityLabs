# LockBit Threat Actor Profile

By Ronisha Showers · Independent portfolio project


## Project Overview
This intelligence profile examines LockBit's motivations, targets, attack methods, and organizational risk using public government reporting and cybersecurity research.
## Assessment Focus
- Threat-actor identity, motivation, and objectives
- Target industries and victimology
- Attack methods and observable behaviors
- Organizational exposure and defensive recommendations
- Intelligence gaps and limits in the available evidence
## Skills Demonstrated
**Threat-actor research, source evaluation, adversary analysis, intelligence-gap identification, risk assessment, and intelligence writing.**
